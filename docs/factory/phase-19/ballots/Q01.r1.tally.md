## Eliminated
- A (user id alone): verified hard objection (architect) — the allowlist is a list (breakdown W02), user ids are unique
  only per workspace, and `resolve` returns the existing owner of `(SLACK, external_id)`, so two admitted workspaces
  can map two people to one account and its knowledge bases. No ballot voted A.
- C' (`team:user`): verified hard objection (architect, skeptic) — `:` in the local part of
  `slack-<id>@users.invalid` makes an address that is not a plain dot-atom; it breaks the ADR 0027 email rule.
- D (enterprise or team + user): no votes; no hard objection needed (removed by rule 3.3).
- E: no votes.
