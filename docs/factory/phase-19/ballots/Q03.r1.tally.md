## Options merged
All three ballots added an option E that is not in the question. The three E rows are the same in substance: take
`KnowledgeBaseService.list(userId)`; exactly one -> use its `kbId`, pass it through `requireOwner(kbId, userId)`, then
work; zero or more than one -> one fixed refusal; no modal, no name, no stored selection; selection is designed with
linking (#43). Merged under E. E differs from B (B shows a modal when the user owns several; E refuses) and from D (D
resolves by name or a stored pointer; E resolves by owner-filtered list and id).
