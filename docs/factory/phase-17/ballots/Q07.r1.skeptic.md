## Reasoning
- Hash over extracted text is stable, so dedup works; raw HTML carries nonces and timestamps (C fails).
- URL in sourceFilename needs no migration and shows provenance in citations; the 1000-char cut is safe.
- Failure modes for A: the title is untrusted; strip control characters, collapse whitespace and cap its length before LessonIdentity.
- If title and filename fallback both fail, skip and log; never a placeholder.
- Hash must be computed after D05 conversion; the conversion must be deterministic.
- A truncated URL could collide on display only; dedup uses the hash, so it is harmless.
- Reusing the IngestionService transaction keeps lock, dedup and the kbId-first tenancy; no model call in the transaction.
## What would change my mind
Evidence that provenance must be queryable or exported by URL; then B.
