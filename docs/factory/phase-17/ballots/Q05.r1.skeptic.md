## Reasoning
- Failure modes of C: malformed HTML, nested script/style, comments, unclosed tags, entities; each bug leaks markup or boilerplate into Extract.
- B widens the upload attack surface (new user-supplied format on all channels) without a requirement.
- A is robust, a single MIT dependency, removable; converter stays infrastructure-private and testable with plain fixtures.
- Test: fixtures for script/style removal, broken HTML, headings, pre/code, title, and no raw markup in output text.
## What would change my mind
