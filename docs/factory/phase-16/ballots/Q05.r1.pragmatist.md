| B  | A + maxImagesPerDocument in ProcessingSettings, fail loudly over cap | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 |

## Reasoning
- No cap means nothing is truncated, so the "fail loudly, never truncate" rule in ai_agents.md is met by design. A cap adds a setting, a failure mode and a test, with no measured need.
- Min-size and hash dedup are cheap, deterministic filters in the parser; they remove the logo/header cost in Q05 context.
- Dropped non-raster (EMF/WMF/SVG) and sub-64px images are a documented filter, not a truncation. Log them at INFO with a count so they are visible (a run counter is optional).
- ImageIO is in the JDK: no new dependency (conventions.md). GIF/BMP/TIFF decode via ImageIO; JPX/JBIG2 depend on PDFBox decode; on decode failure skip the image and log WARN.
- Opt-out switch: skip. Without a configured VISION model the describer fails loudly per D01; add a switch only when an operator needs one. Easy to add later.
- Per-image byte cap: re-encode check only; if the encoded image exceeds a fixed constant (e.g. 5 MB) skip it with a counted log. Keep as a constant in the parser, not a setting.
- Dedup by hash at parse time (keep first, drop repeats) keeps D09 document order for the kept block.

## What would change my mind
Measured runs with hundreds of images per document (cost/permit starvation) would justify B with a counted omission.
