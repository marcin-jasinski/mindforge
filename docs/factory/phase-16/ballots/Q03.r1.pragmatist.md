## Options
| id | option | correct | fit | simple | risk | reversible |
|----|--------|---------|-----|--------|------|------------|
| A  | Abstract overload `complete(ModelTier, String prompt, byte[] image, String mimeType, DeadlineProfile)`; adapter trusts the tier | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| B  | Abstract overload with domain record `ImageInput(byte[] data, String mimeType)` | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| C  | One method with `List<ImageInput>` attachments; old method delegates with empty list | 5 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| D  | A or B, plus the adapter rejects a non-VISION tier with an image (IllegalArgumentException) | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| E  | New method as a `default` on `AIGateway` (implementors override only if needed) | 3 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 |

## Reasoning
- The port has one method and exactly four implementors, none of them lambdas (grep "implements AIGateway":
  AIGatewayAdapter, PermitGateway, TracingGateway, StubAIGateway). An abstract overload is a small, compiler-checked
  change: each decorator gets a 5-line copy of its existing method, so permit and span are guaranteed (rules out E).
- A adds zero new types. The image and MIME type are only ever produced together by one caller (`ImageDescriber`)
  and consumed by one adapter that converts them to a Spring AI `Media` at once; a record (B) is one more domain
  type for a single call site. `minimal-implementation.md`: "Skip factories, strategies, or adapters unless there's
  an immediate need." B is a fine second choice if the panel prefers a named value; the cost difference is small.
- C is speculative generality: the question itself says "nobody needs several images per call today", and it
  touches the existing text method in every implementor.
- Single-shot is kept: one `chatModel.call(Prompt)` with one `UserMessage` carrying text + Media goes through the same
  `callWithDeadline` (Retry + CircuitBreaker inside the deadline race), so ADR 0013's "unchanged" holds in substance.
- Tier guard (D): the tier is a constant in `ImageDescriber` and `ImageDescriberTest` asserts VISION on the recorded
  call; a guard adds a rule and a test for a misuse no caller can make. I would accept a one-line guard if the
  skeptic wants it; it is cheap and reversible, but not needed to meet the requirement.
